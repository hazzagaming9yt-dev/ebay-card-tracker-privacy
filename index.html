Privacy Policy — Hazza Pokémon Tracker / eBay Card Tracker

Last Updated: 20 September 2026

1. About This Application

Hazza Pokémon Tracker / eBay Card Tracker ("the application") is a personal application used to monitor and analyse Pokémon trading-card listings and the owner's own eBay selling activity through eBay APIs.

2. Information Accessed

The application may access and store information related to the owner's eBay listings and sales, including:

  • Listing IDs, titles, and listing status
  • Prices, postage costs, quantities, and listing dates
  • Views, impressions, and watcher counts
  • Click-through rate and conversion metrics
  • Completed transactions and orders
  • Quantities sold and sales revenue

This information may be associated with internal Pokémon Card IDs used in the owner's private Google Sheets card tracker.

3. Buyer Information

Buyer names, email addresses, postal addresses, payment information, and other buyer-identifying information are not intentionally stored by the seller analytics system.

4. How Information Is Used

Information obtained through eBay APIs is used to:

  • Monitor active and completed listings
  • Measure listing performance and trends
  • Track sales, quantities sold, and revenue
  • Associate eBay listings with Pokémon cards in the owner's tracker
  • Create historical statistics and dashboards

Information obtained through eBay APIs is not sold, rented, or used for advertising.

5. Data Storage

Listing and seller analytics information may be stored in a private Google Sheets workbook controlled by the application owner.

OAuth credentials and refresh tokens are stored separately from the visible spreadsheet and are used only to authenticate authorised API requests.

Railway may be used to host application pages and OAuth-related endpoints.

6. Sharing

Information is not intentionally shared with third parties except where necessary to operate the application and its supporting services, including eBay, Google, and Railway.

7. eBay Account Access

Access to eBay is granted through eBay's OAuth authorization system. The application does not receive or store the user's eBay password. Authorization may be revoked by the account owner through eBay account or authorization settings.

8. Data Retention

Historical listing and sales statistics may be retained to support long-term analysis. Information may be removed when it is no longer required or when the integration is discontinued.

9. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to restrict access to stored information and authentication credentials. API credentials and OAuth tokens are not intended to be stored in publicly accessible source code.

10. Affiliation

This application is an independent personal project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by eBay Inc. or The Pokémon Company. All trademarks belong to their respective owners.

11. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy or the application's data practices may be directed to the application owner.
